Features used in the crop yield model:

Numeric Features:
1. Avg_Temperature(°C)
2. Rainfall(mm)
3. humidity
4. ph
5. nitrogen
6. phosphorus
7. potassium

Categorical Features:
1. soil_type
   Values: Clay, Sandy, Loamy, Black, Red, Silt, Chalky, Peat
2. crop_type
   Values: Rice, Wheat, Maize, Potato, Tomato, Cotton, Sugarcane, Coffee, Tea, Jute
3. pesticide_use
   Values: Low, Medium, High
4. region
   Values: Kigali, Eastern, Western, Northern, Southern
