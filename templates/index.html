Ibiranga by'ingenzi

Bikoreshwa Ubwenge Buhanga

Imashini zacu zigezweho zo kwiga imashini zisesengura ibintu byinshi kugirango zitange umusaruro ushimishije mubihe byihariye.

Isesengura ry'akarere

Kubona amakuru yihariye ashingiye ku biranga akarere kagufi ka Rwanda n'ibihe by'umwihariko.

Ukobimeze imyaka yashize

Gerageza kwiyumvisha imikorere y'ibihingwa mugihe kugirango umenye imiterere kandi uhindure gahunda yo gutera.

Ikirere

Harimo igihe-nyacyo kandi cyateganijwe amakuru yikirere kugirango arusheho guhanura.

Amakuru Yifashishwa

Kwakira inama mu bikorwa kugira ngo umusaruro wawe wongerwe ushingiye ku byavuye mu guteganiriza.

Ikoresha Terefone

Kugera ku biranga byose kuri terefone, byiza cyane ku bahinzi bo mu mirima bafite uburyo buke bwa tekiniki.

Uko Bikora

Porogaramu yacu yo guteganiriza ihuriza hamwe amakuru atandukanye n'imashini ziherereye kugira ngo itange umusaruro wizewe

Intambwe 1
Gukusanya Amakuru

Dukusanya amakuru y'umusaruro washize, imiterere y'ikirere, ibihe by'ubutaka, n'imikorere y'ubuhinzi mu Rwanda hose.

Intambwe 2
Gutoza Imashini

Imashini zacu zo kwiga zitojwe kuri aya makuru kugira ngo zimenyekane imiterere n'umubano hagati y'amakuru atandukanye.

Intambwe 3
Amakuru y'Ukoresha

Utanga ibisobanuro ku bibazo byawe byihariye - ubwoko bw'igihingwa, ahantu, n'ibihe witeze.

Intambwe 4
Gutanga Umuteganyo

Sisitemu yacu isesengura ibyo watanze binyuze mu moderi yigishijwe kugira ngo itange umuteganyo w'umusaruro wihariye.

Intambwe 5
Ibisobanuro & Inama

Uhabwa umuteganyondetse n'amakuru afatika kugira ngo agufashe kongera umusaruro nyakuri.

Intego Yacu
